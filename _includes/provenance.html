Provenance

Every version of this site is content-addressed and pinned to IPFS. That means each build produces a unique, verifiable fingerprint — a CID — that cannot be altered without changing the address itself. Words here cannot be quietly revised and denied.

Git commits provide a second layer: a timestamped, cryptographically linked chain of every change. The history is public and immutable. What was written is what was written.

This is not paranoia. It is architecture. Truth deserves infrastructure.
