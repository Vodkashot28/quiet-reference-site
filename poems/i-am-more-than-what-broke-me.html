I Am More Than What Broke Me

Beyond the worst moment — the quiet rebuilding no one applauded.

I am not defined by the worst thing I did. I am not confined to the moment I lost control. I am not reduced to the story told without me. I am more than what broke me. I am the repair. I am the persistence. I am the quiet rebuilding no one applauded. I am the father who stayed. I am the man who learned. I am the soul who chose dignity over destruction. I am not perfect. But I am whole. And that wholeness is mine to keep.
