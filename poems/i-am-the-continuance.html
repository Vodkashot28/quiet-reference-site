I am not the end. I am not the collapse. I am not the ruin. I am the continuance. I am the thread that holds together what others tried to tear apart. I am the persistence that keeps walking, keeps breathing, keeps choosing peace. I am the quiet that rebuilds. I am the legacy that endures.
