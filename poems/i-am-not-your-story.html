I Am Not Your Story

Reclaiming identity from the version others chose to tell.

You told them who I was. You painted me with your colors, your anger, your fear. But I am not your story. I am not your version. I am not the character you needed me to play. I am my own witness. I am my own truth. I am my own ending. And I will not live inside your script.
