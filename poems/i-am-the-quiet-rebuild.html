I Am the Quiet Rebuild

The quiet rebuilding no one witnessed — done not for recognition, but for peace.

No one applauded. No one noticed. No one asked. But I rebuilt anyway. Brick by brick, moment by moment, truth by truth. I rebuilt quietly. Not for recognition. Not for forgiveness. But for peace. And that rebuild is mine to keep.
