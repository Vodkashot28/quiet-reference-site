Boundaries are not punishment. They are protection. They are clarity. They are survival. I draw them not in anger, but in dignity. I draw them not to divide, but to preserve. I am the keeper of boundaries. I am the guardian of peace. I am the witness to my own worth. And I will not let anyone cross what I have claimed as sacred.
