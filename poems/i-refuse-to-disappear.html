I Refuse to Disappear

On refusing to vanish into blame, silence, or the shadows others cast.

I will not vanish into blame, into the shadows others cast. I will not shrink to make peace with lies. I will not erase myself to keep the roof steady. I remain. Not as a ghost, not as a wound, but as a voice that insists on being heard.
